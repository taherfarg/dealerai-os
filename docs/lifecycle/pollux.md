# Pollux Motors, from its own website

What [polluxmotors.com](https://polluxmotors.com) says about the business, read on 2026-11-04,
and what it changes in the lifecycle plan ([README.md](README.md)). The site is the source; where
a fact below is not on it, it says so.

## The business

- **New cars.** "A wide selection of brand-new vehicles from all major brands" — every listing read
  is a 2025 or 2026 model with 0 km. Not used cars, and nothing on the site about auctions.
- **Chinese-spec and GCC-spec.** Many listings are Chinese-market cars (BYD Destroyer 05, Nissan
  N6, Kia Sportage L — "Chinese Specs"), beside GCC-spec ones (Toyota Hilux Travo, Dongfeng A30).
  Brands: premium (Mercedes-Benz, BMW, Audi, Range Rover, Porsche, Lexus, Volkswagen), global
  (Toyota, Mitsubishi, Nissan, Hyundai, KIA, Suzuki), electric and Chinese (BYD, Geely, Tesla,
  Changan, Chery).
- **Where.** Office WS 12, DAZ Business Center 2, DUCAMZ (Dubai Auto Zone), Ras Al Khor, Dubai.
  Showroom Mon–Sat 10:00–20:00, viewings by appointment. Est. 2022.
- **Scale, as the site states it.** 10K+ vehicles delivered, 50+ countries, 97% client retention.
- **Services.** New cars; UAE and export transportation; RTA registration; shipping and clearance;
  GSO and certificate-of-origin documentation.
- **Markets.** Export to the GCC, Europe (UK, Germany, France), Africa (Egypt, Algeria, Tunisia,
  Libya, Morocco, Kenya, Nigeria, South Africa), Asia and the Americas. The site is in English,
  Arabic and **French** — the North African markets.
- **Its own AI.** `/ai` is a customer-facing assistant (find, compare, export planning) in the same
  three languages. It is a lead source this platform should eventually receive leads from.

## How an export buyer pays

From the export FAQ: "a deposit to reserve the vehicle, the balance on confirmation of inspection
and documentation, and final logistics costs as the car ships. Every invoice separates the vehicle
price, fees, freight, and duties." No bank finance is mentioned for export buyers.

## The export procedures, by destination

From `/export-services`. Durations exclude sea freight.

**Algeria — 11 steps, about 5–8 business days**

1. Request documents from the customer: passport copy, recipient information, delivery address
2. Assign the vehicle; verify its condition and all accessories
3. Agree the shipping date, cost and details with the shipping company; confirm with the customer
4. Export inspection, and the export inspection certificate, via RTA
5. Deliver the vehicle to the shipping company
6. Request the export certificate from the RTA website
7. Confirm the bill of lading draft with the customer
8. Receive the original bill of lading (2–4 days)
9. Issue the Dubai Chamber of Commerce certificate
10. Issue the commercial invoice
11. Compile the file and deliver it to the customer

**Tunisia — 11 steps.** As Algeria, with **Dubai export plates registered at RTA after the
inspection** (step 5), and no Dubai Chamber certificate.

**Other countries — 9 steps, about 6–9 business days.** Customer documents; assign and verify;
**the Vehicle Clearance Certificate (customs card)**; agree shipping; RTA export certificate; bill
of lading draft, then the original; commercial invoice; compile and deliver.

Shipping from Jebel Ali: full container (20 ft, 40 ft, 40 ft HC; 2–6 weeks), shared container, or
RoRo (3–5 weeks); air freight for urgent cases (3–7 days). Every car gets a pre-shipment
inspection with photos, a video walkaround and notes; for an EV, battery state of health,
charging standard for the destination and software version.
