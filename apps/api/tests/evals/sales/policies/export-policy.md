# Export policy

Pollux Motors exports new and used vehicles from Dubai to North Africa, West
Africa and the Gulf. This policy is the dealership's own and is what our sales
team quotes.

## Shipping

Vehicles leave from Jebel Ali. Roll-on roll-off sailings run weekly on Tuesday;
container sailings run twice a month. Transit is about fourteen days to Algiers,
eighteen to Casablanca and twenty-five to Dakar. Shipping is arranged by us and
billed at cost; the customer may also arrange their own freight forwarder.

## Documents the buyer must provide

A passport copy, a certificate of residence in the destination country, and the
consignee's full name and address in Latin letters. For a company purchase we
also need the trade licence. Annex B lists the customs codes each destination
requires on the commercial invoice.

## Customs and duty

Customs duty is paid by the buyer on arrival and is never included in our price.
Algeria charges duty on the CIF value. We issue the certificate of origin and
the commercial invoice; we do not clear customs on the buyer's behalf.

## Countries we do not ship to

We do not ship to countries under sanction, and we do not ship left-hand-drive
vehicles to Kenya, Uganda or Tanzania, which require right-hand drive.
