# Financing

## Who can finance a car with us

Residents of the UAE with a valid Emirates ID and three months of salary
transfers. Visitors and customers buying for export cannot finance; those
purchases are cash or bank transfer.

## Down payment and instalments

The minimum down payment is 20% of the vehicle price. Terms run from twelve to
sixty months. The monthly instalment depends on the bank's rate on the day of
approval, so we never quote a monthly figure before the bank has answered.

## How long approval takes

Two to three working days once the bank has the documents. We submit on the
customer's behalf. An approval is the bank's decision and not ours, and nobody
at Pollux can promise one.

## Paying by bank transfer

Transfers must come from an account in the buyer's own name. We release a
vehicle when the funds have cleared, not when the transfer is shown.
