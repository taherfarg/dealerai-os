# Warranty and trade-in

## Warranty on a new vehicle

New vehicles carry the manufacturer's warranty, which is three years or 100,000
kilometres, whichever comes first, and it is honoured by the manufacturer's
network in the UAE. Exported vehicles carry the warranty only where the
manufacturer supports it in the destination country.

## Warranty on a used vehicle

Used vehicles under five years old carry a six-month powertrain warranty from
Pollux Motors covering engine and gearbox. Vehicles sold as-is are marked as-is
on the invoice.

## Trading in your car

We take trade-ins against any vehicle we sell. The car must be inspected at the
showroom before we can put a figure on it; nobody can value a car from a
photograph or a description. Bring the registration card and the service history.

## Service history and inspection

An inspection takes about forty minutes and is free. We check the chassis,
service history and accident record through the registration authority.
